# Frequency 303 starter samples

32 unmodified WAV recordings from Frequency 303, published by Allan Legemaate,
retrieved 30 October 2026. No claim of affiliation or manufacturer endorsement.

- **MS-20 Drums:** 16 selected kick, clap, snare and hi-hat hits.
  Source: https://www.frequency303.com/samples/ms20-drums/
  Archive: https://storage.googleapis.com/www-frequency303-com-data-prod/MS-20-Drums.zip
- **Synth FX:** ten alarms, filter sweeps, laser effects and a whoosh, plus six
  synth/bass sounds sampled at C3.
  Source: https://www.frequency303.com/samples/ms20-samples/
  Archive: https://storage.googleapis.com/www-frequency303-com-data-prod/MS-20-Samples-AllanLegemaate.zip

SOURCES.csv maps every bundled WAV to its original archive path, display name
and SHA-256 digest. Audio bytes are unchanged. Display names and disk filenames
have been shortened. Original DirectWave presets and unrelated files are omitted.

The application seeds editable copies of these banks once on first use of its
sampler directory. Your existing bank is retained as My Samples. Changing or
clearing a starter slot thereafter is preserved on subsequent launches. Source
recordings remain available here for rebuilding a bank. Samples play at their
recorded pitch; these are one-shots and effects, not tempo-synchronised loops.
